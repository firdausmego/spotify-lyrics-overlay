♪ Spotify Lyrics Overlay

Transparent desktop overlay

  1. Start the companion overlay app (see the README).
  2. Play something on open.spotify.com.
  3. Lyrics appear on your desktop as text only. Hover them for playback controls.

No app installed?

  1. Click the green ♪ button in the Spotify tab, or press Alt+L.
  2. A small always-on-top lyrics window opens. It stays visible when you switch tabs or minimize Chrome.

Lyrics are provided by LRCLIB. GitHub
